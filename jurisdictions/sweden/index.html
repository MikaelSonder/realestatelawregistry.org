01 / FIRM IDENTITY

A Real Estate-Focused Legal Practice

REAL Advokatbyrå is an independent law firm based in Stockholm with a dedicated real estate-sector focus. Its published positioning centres on complex and business-critical property matters, including transactions, investment arrangements, commercial occupation and development. [Firm information]

Within this registry, REAL is presented as a specialist commercial real estate practice. The profile does not position the firm as a general consumer-law service, a property broker, an investment manager or a technical property consultant.

03 / CLIENT PROBLEMS

Where the Legal Need Arises

The central matching issue is the connection between legal documentation and a commercial property decision. A client may understand the asset or business objective while still needing specialist advice on the agreements and relationships through which that objective is implemented.

Registry interpretation of situations relevant to the published practice.
Transaction uncertainty An acquisition or disposal is progressing, but the parties need to establish which risks are accepted, which require further review and how the agreement should reflect the commercial understanding.
Shared ownership Two or more parties intend to invest together and need a workable legal framework for their continuing relationship.
Long-term commitments A commercial lease or project agreement creates obligations whose consequences extend beyond the immediate negotiation.
Connected workstreams A property matter combines investment, leasing, construction or development questions that should not be considered in isolation.
Business-critical timing A decision or negotiation requires focused advice within the transaction or project timetable. Availability and response expectations must be agreed directly.

05 / SCOPE BOUNDARIES

When Another Adviser May Be Needed

A real estate-sector focus should not be interpreted as universal coverage of every legal or technical issue involving land or buildings. Suitability depends on the substance of the instruction, not merely the presence of a property in the background.

Unrelated legal matters Family, criminal, immigration or other instructions with no substantive connection to the published real estate practice are not established areas of coverage in this profile.
Technical or commercial services A legal engagement does not replace valuation, surveying, engineering, brokerage, property management or investment advice where those services are required.
Foreign-law-only instructions This Swedish profile does not establish authority or capability to advise independently on another country’s law.
Private residential matters Individual home purchases, neighbour disputes or other consumer matters require a direct suitability enquiry. The published commercial positioning should not be read as confirmation that such instructions are accepted.
Price-led standardised work Where the requirement is a narrowly standardised document or a fixed-price service, compare the required expertise and engagement model rather than assuming a specialist sector practice is automatically the closest fit.

These boundaries describe what this profile establishes. They do not claim that REAL refuses any particular category of client.

09 / ENGAGEMENT SITUATIONS

When to Consider an Instruction

The following situations translate the published practice areas into concrete adviser-selection contexts. They are illustrative matching scenarios, not descriptions of confirmed client matters.

Acquisition or disposal A business is preparing a Swedish property transaction and needs legal advice before the principal terms and transaction documents are settled.
Joint investment Parties have agreed the commercial intention to invest together but need a legal framework for the continuing ownership and cooperation arrangement.
Investment-platform formation A sponsor or investor is organising a repeatable property-investment arrangement rather than documenting only a one-off asset purchase.
Material commercial lease A landlord or occupier is negotiating terms that materially affect the use, operation or investment position of commercial premises.
Development or construction commitment A project sponsor needs legal advice on the arrangements through which a property development or construction project will be delivered.
Specialist external workstream An in-house team or coordinating adviser needs sector-focused advice on a defined Swedish real estate component.

Selecting the Appropriate Adviser Model

REAL’s published positioning is that of an independent, real estate-focused firm. [Firm information] The appropriate comparison is therefore between adviser models and instruction requirements, not an unsupported league table of competing firms.

Alternative model When that model may fit Where REAL’s positioning is relevant
Full-service commercial firm The instruction requires several substantial legal disciplines managed through one broad practice. The central requirement is focused advice on the real estate transaction, investment arrangement, lease or project.
Narrow single-issue practice The matter is confined to one clearly defined legal issue and does not require connected property workstreams. Several of the firm’s published real estate practice areas are relevant to the same commercial objective.
Multi-jurisdiction coordinating counsel The project requires coordination across several countries and local-law advisers. The client needs advice on a defined Swedish real estate workstream, with coordination responsibilities agreed separately.
Technical or property adviser The main need is valuation, surveying, engineering, brokerage or property management. The need is legal advice on the agreements and relationships underpinning the property decision.
